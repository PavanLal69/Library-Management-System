## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: polished
- Creative direction: Distinguished academic library catalog meets high-performance systems engineering
- Interpretation: Pacing is confident, clean, and restrained. Pacing respects reading time (minimum 1.2s settled on sentences). Warm paper/parchment background (`#faf8f5`) with deep charcoal ink typography and subtle gold/amber accents.
- Angle: In a world of fleeting digital links, this platform treats physical books like mission-critical hardware: tracking physical volumes down through 7 levels of physical campus shelving with ACID circulation locks.
- Hook: The question "What would you like to read today?" typing out "Designing Data-Intensive Applications".
- Outro / punchline: "From distributed consensus to the exact physical shelf."
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS language
  - Abstract filler visuals
  - Unrelated visual redesign
